# KineWorld brand guidance

The supplied blue logo is the source of truth: `public/assets/logo-original.jpg`. The transparent PNG and favicon are derived by `scripts/prepare_assets.py`; review them against the original after any source change. Preserve the outer silhouette, interior negative space, proportions and blue identity. “Four-dimensional” describes the intended spatial feeling, not a strict mathematical projection.

The supplied portal image is `public/assets/portal-original.png`. It represents passage from observation toward understanding and from the present toward possible futures. The optimized WebP and Open Graph JPEG preserve the scene. Do not replace the portal with a generic rectangular border.

The site also uses four generated backgrounds saved with their source PNGs in `public/assets/`: `world-spatial` for the homepage, `world-action` for research, `world-landscape` for projects and `portal-variant` for the About/closing brand narrative. The three world-model visuals are conceptual illustrations, not photographs of KineWorld experiments or model output. Do not caption them as experimental evidence.

Color: #05070B base, #07101B secondary, #006BFF blue identity, #2F8FFF accent, #F5F7FA text, #96A5B7 secondary text. Prefer darkness, negative space and measured blue light. The site uses system Chinese and Latin sans-serif fonts; no commercial font files are redistributed.

Media assets may be downloaded from `/media/`. The original JPG remains available for comparison.
